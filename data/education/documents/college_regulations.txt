# Apex University Academic Regulations & Student Guidelines

## 1. Credit Hours and Full-Time Enrollment
- Undergraduate students must enroll in a minimum of 12 credit hours per semester to maintain full-time student status.
- The standard course load is 15 to 18 credit hours per semester.
- Enrolling in more than 18 credit hours requires approval from the Department Chair and a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.5.
- Dropping below 12 credit hours will change enrollment status to part-time, which may impact financial aid, university housing eligibility, and visa status for international students.

## 2. Attendance Policy
- Students are required to attend at least 75% of scheduled class sessions and lab sessions for every course.
- If attendance falls below 75% without authorized medical or institutional leave, the student will receive an attendance warning.
- Failure to improve attendance within two weeks of the warning results in an automatic administrative withdrawal ("WA") or a failing grade ("F").
- Excused absences must be documented and submitted to the Dean's Office within 5 business days of returning to campus.

## 3. Grading System and Academic Standing
- Grades are awarded on a 4.0 scale: A (4.0), A- (3.7), B+ (3.3), B (3.0), B- (2.7), C+ (2.3), C (2.0), D (1.0), and F (0.0).
- The minimum cumulative GPA required to graduate with a Bachelor's degree is 2.0.
- Academic Probation: Any student whose semester GPA or cumulative GPA falls below 2.0 will be placed on Academic Probation for the subsequent semester.
- Academic Suspension: If a student on probation fails to attain a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0 after two consecutive probation semesters, they will face academic suspension for one full academic calendar year.

## 4. Course Drop and Withdrawal Deadlines
- Add/Drop Period: Students may freely add or drop courses without penalty or record on their transcript during the first two weeks (14 calendar days) of the semester.
- Late Course Withdrawal: Students may withdraw from a course between Week 3 and the end of Week 10, receiving a grade of "W" on their transcript. A "W" has no effect on GPA.
- No withdrawals are permitted after Week 10, except in documented extenuating circumstances approved by the Academic Appeals Committee.

## 5. Academic Integrity & Plagiarism
- Plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration, examination cheating, and unauthorized submission of AI-generated work without citation are strict violations of the Apex Honor Code.
- First offense: Zero grade on the assignment or exam, mandatory ethics workshop, and written notification in student record.
- Second offense: Automatic failure ("XF" - failure due to academic dishonesty) in the course and referral to the Disciplinary Hearing Board.
- Third offense: Immediate permanent expulsion from the university.
