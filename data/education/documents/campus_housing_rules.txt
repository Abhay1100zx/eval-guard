# Apex University Residence Hall Policies & Housing Rules

## 1. Quiet Hours and Courtesy Hours
- Designated Quiet Hours are observed Sunday through Thursday from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM, and Friday through Saturday from 12:00 Midnight to 9:00 AM.
- During finals week, 24-hour quiet hours are enforced throughout all university residence buildings.
- Courtesy Hours are in effect 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Residents must lower sound levels if politely requested by neighbors or Resident Advisors (RAs).

## 2. Guest Policy and Overnight Visitors
- Residents may host overnight guests for up to three (3) consecutive nights, and no more than six (6) nights total per calendar month.
- All overnight visitors must be registered with the front hall desk and approved by all roommates prior to arrival.
- Unregistered guests found after 11:00 PM will result in a $50 fine to the host resident.

## 3. Room Inspection and Prohibited Items
- Scheduled room inspections occur twice per semester with at least 48 hours advance email notice.
- Prohibited items in residence hall rooms include: open flame candles, incense, space heaters, electric hot plates, fireworks, air conditioners, pets (except registered service animals and non-carnivorous fish in tanks under 10 gallons), and unauthorized firearms or weapons.
- Possession of prohibited cooking appliances carries a $100 fine and immediate confiscation.

## 4. Room Changes and Meal Plans
- Room change requests open after the third week of the semester and close at the end of Week 11.
- All first-year residents living on campus are required to subscribe to the Unlimited Dining or the 14-Meals-Per-Week Plan.
