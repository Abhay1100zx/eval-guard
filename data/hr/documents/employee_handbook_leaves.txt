# Apex Global Enterprise: Employee Handbook, Benefits & Workplace Policies

## 1. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Accrual
- Annual Allotment: Full-time employees receive eighteen (18) days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued at 1.5 days per completed calendar month of active service.
- Rollover Policy: A maximum of five (5) unused PTO days may roll over into the subsequent calendar year. Rollover days must be consumed prior to March 31 or they will expire.
- Sick Leave: Employees receive ten (10) standalone paid sick days annually, accessible immediately upon hire, which do not accrue and cannot be cashed out upon separation.

## 2. Parental Leave & Family Care
- Primary Caregiver Leave: Sixteen (16) weeks of 100% fully paid parental leave for birth, adoption, or foster placement. Eligible after six (6) months of continuous service.
- Secondary Caregiver Leave: Eight (8) weeks of 100% fully paid parental leave, usable continuously or in increments within twelve (12) months of arrival.
- Bereavement Leave: Up to five (5) consecutive paid business days for immediate family members (spouse, child, parent, sibling).

## 3. Remote Work & Equipment Stipends
- Home Office Setup Stipend: One-time lump sum reimbursement of $500 for ergonomics, desk, monitors, or office furniture submitted within ninety (90) days of hiring.
- Connectivity Allowance: Monthly recurring payroll stipend of $50 for high-speed home internet and mobile communications.

## 4. Performance Reviews and Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)
- Performance Cycles: Formal reviews occur twice annually in June (mid-year check-in) and December (annual compensation review).
- Performance Improvement Plan: Employees placed on a formal PIP have sixty (60) calendar days to meet clearly delineated metrics with weekly manager checkpoints.
- Code of Conduct & Anti-Harassment: Zero-tolerance policy for discriminatory harassment or retaliation. Reports can be filed 24/7 anonymously via EthicsPoint hotline.
