# GrandHorizon Resorts & Travel: Hospitality, Booking & Guest Policies

## 1. Check-In, Check-Out and Luggage Policies
- Standard Check-In: 3:00 PM local time. Early check-in before 1:00 PM is subject to availability and a $35 early arrival fee.
- Standard Check-Out: 11:00 AM local time. Late check-out between 12:00 PM and 3:00 PM is billed at $25 per hour. Check-outs after 3:00 PM incur a full night's room charge.
- Luggage Storage: Complimentary luggage holding is provided for up to twelve (12) hours on the day of arrival and departure.

## 2. Cancellation, Deposit and No-Show Policies
- Flexible Rate Cancellation: Reservations may be cancelled without penalty up to forty-eight (48) hours prior to the 3:00 PM check-in date.
- Late Cancellation & No-Show Penalty: Cancellations within 48 hours or failure to arrive on the scheduled date result in a forfeiture penalty equal to one (1) night room rate plus taxes.
- Non-Refundable Promotional Bookings: Fully pre-paid at booking and ineligible for any refund or date modification.

## 3. Pet Policies and Smoking Restrictions
- Pet Accommodation: Guests may bring up to two (2) domestic dogs weighing under 50 pounds each. A non-refundable pet cleaning fee of $50 per stay applies.
- Service Animals: Fully certified ADA service animals stay free of charge without size restrictions or pet fees.
- Smoking Penalty: All indoor rooms and balconies are 100% non-smoking. A $300 deep-cleaning sanitization fee is automatically billed for violations.

## 4. Airline Delays, Meal Vouchers and Disruptions
- Flight Delay Compensation: If a connecting charter transfer is delayed more than four (4) hours due to airline scheduling, guests receive a $50 meal voucher per passenger.
- Overnight Mechanical Grounding: In the event of flight cancellations resulting from technical or mechanical aircraft issues, complimentary standard hotel lodging and round-trip airport shuttles are provided.
