# NovaStore E-Commerce: Shipping, Returns & Customer Service Policy

## 1. Shipping Methods, Delivery Times and Costs
- Standard Shipping: Delivers in 3 to 5 business days. Free for all orders with a subtotal of $50 or more; otherwise a flat rate of $4.99 applies.
- Express 2-Day Shipping: $12.99 flat rate for guaranteed 2-business-day delivery.
- Overnight Delivery: $24.99 flat rate. Orders must be submitted prior to 1:00 PM EST Monday through Thursday for next-day delivery.
- Order Modifications & Cancellations: Customers may cancel or modify order shipping details within sixty (60) minutes of order placement directly through the self-service portal.

## 2. Return Policy & Guidelines
- Return Window: Customers may return eligible products within thirty (30) days from the delivery date for a full refund.
- Item Condition: Items must be in original condition, unwashed, unworn, and with all original tags attached. Original packaging is required for electronics and footwear.
- Non-Returnable Items: Final sale items, personalized products, gift cards, and intimate apparel/swimwear without hygiene seals cannot be returned.
- Return Shipping Fee: Returns for store credit are 100% free with a prepaid label. Returns requesting refund to the original payment method incur a $5.99 return shipping deduction.

## 3. Refunds & Replacements
- Processing Time: Once received at the fulfillment center, inspections are completed within 48 hours. Refunds are credited back to original payment methods within 5 to 7 business days.
- Store Credit Bonus: Customers opting for store credit instead of a bank refund receive an additional 5% bonus credit applied immediately to their NovaStore balance.

## 4. Damaged, Defective, or Lost Shipments
- Damaged on Arrival: Customers must report damaged or defective merchandise within seven (7) days of delivery with photographic proof to receive an instant free replacement.
- Lost Packages: If a tracking status indicates delivered but package is missing, customers must report within fourteen (14) days of delivery date.
